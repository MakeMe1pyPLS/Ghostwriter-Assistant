# ChainInsideIQ

An AI-powered business tool for companies of every size, from local shops to enterprises, and the consultants who help them. Owners tell the AI Analyst their goal and get a step-by-step growth plan. The analyst spots the bottlenecks holding the business back, and teams fix them together with the suppliers, carriers and partners they work with. Dashboards can be built, generated or enhanced, then exported to Excel, Power BI, Google Sheets and more.

Powered by three AI engines (Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini) with a built-in analyst as a fallback.

- Frontend: React 19, Vite, Tailwind 4, Radix UI, Framer Motion
- Backend: Express 5 (Node.js)
- Shared logic (`shared/`): KPI library, growth plans, rule-based analyst, exporters

See `PHASE-1-CHANGES.md` for setup, environment variables and deployment.
